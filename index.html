KARMAN

Physically based, procedural worlds — from orbit down to a single blade of grass.

A tour of the system

One continuous reel across five worlds: E-geohistory from orbit to the valley floor, Genesis's coast and canyon country, alpine ridges in cloud, Umber's summit snow and violet forest, the rainforest, down through Vitriol's acid deck, Vesper's craters at low sun, and an eclipse.

Geohistory

A living planet shaped by deep time: rain-cut valleys, cloud decks, and forests that follow the climate from desert to alpine.

Umber

An alien world of rust-red soil, where thorny, berry-laden plants replace the familiar forests.

Vitriol

A scalding world under a sky of sulfuric-acid cloud, where dark, waxy plants cling to the cooler heights.

Vesper

A small airless moon, grey and silent, its dust pocked by craters of every age under a black sky.

Cronus

An icy moon inside a vast ring system, every ringlet and gap traced from a real spacecraft's measurements of Saturn.

Genesis

A sun-baked canyon world of layered sandstone, wind-carved arches and a bright, glittering sea.

Braudor

A generated world: temperate continents, from ice caps to rainforest.

Verlen

A generated world: a warm ocean world whose islands span tundra to tropical forest.

Selene

The Moon's twin: bright cratered highlands and dark lava seas, every unit from fresh rays to cold polar traps.

Rime

A Ganymede-like ice moon: dark, ancient cratered ice and younger bright plains where slushy ice flooded the lows.

Tovik

An icy moon under an orange star: dark cratered ice with smooth plains flooded from below.
